About some main characters

Captain Luffy, with his rubber body, is cheerful and aims to be King of the Pirates.

Swordsman Zoro fights with three swords and strives to be the world's best.

Sanji, the Straw Hat cook, fights with powerful kicks and loves women. He dreams of the All Blue.

Robin, the archaeologist, has the Flower-Flower Fruit and seeks the world's lost history.
